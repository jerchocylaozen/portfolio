how i work

Five steps from your footage to a finished video, each one with the shortcut I press for it.

M

Brief

You tell me the goal, the platform and the style you want. I mark the moments that matter.

Ctrl+I

Footage

You send the raw clips, music and any references, and I import them.

Ctrl+B

Rough cut

I cut the story and pacing, then send you a first version.

Space

Polish

I play it back again and again while captions, color, sound and your feedback go in.

Export

Deliver

I export the final file, sized for where you're posting it.

Shortcuts shown are from CapCut, the editor I use most: add marker, import, split, play and export. I also edit in DaVinci Resolve.
